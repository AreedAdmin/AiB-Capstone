# Consulting Project — Company Shortlist & Ranking

**Prepared:** 2026-05-17
**Source:** `teams.txt` (MSc Business Analytics – Consulting Project Briefs 2026)

## Ranking Methodology

Each company is scored on two criteria (1–10):

1. **Company Size & Reputation** — brand recognition and prestige we could leverage on a CV, in interviews, and for our professional network. *(Weighted higher per the brief.)*
2. **Project Technicality & Complexity** — depth of Python / machine learning / deep learning / data-heavy work, vs. pure strategy/market-research.

**Weighting:** Reputation **60%**, Technicality **40%**. The aim is a prestigious name *and* a genuinely technical, data-heavy project — but when forced to trade off, name wins.

> Note: Several firms submitted two briefs (CeramTec, Ekimetrics, Strivolabs). They are listed once as a company, with the strongest brief flagged.

## Top 10 Ranked

### 1. CRISIL Coalition Greenwich (an S&P Global company) — ⭐ Top Pick
- **Reputation: 9/10** · **Technicality: 9/10** · **Weighted: 9.0**
- **Why #1:** Best balance of both criteria. "S&P Global / CRISIL" is a blue-chip, globally recognised name in financial data and analytics — exceptional leverage. The project is also the most genuinely data-science-heavy on the list: 20+ years of deep proprietary financial data across thousands of data points, explicitly asking for AI/ML, econometric modelling, and near-term forecasting, with "free rein" to extract insights. Open-ended, technical, and prestigious.

### 2. RBC (RBC BlueBay / RBC Global Asset Management)
- **Reputation: 9/10** · **Technicality: 8/10** · **Weighted: 8.6**
- **Why:** RBC is one of the largest banks globally — a tier-1 finance brand. The project is at the frontier of applied AI: integrating LLMs into an active equity investment process (earnings-call/filing synthesis, ESG NLP on unstructured data, healthcare-sector deep-dive). Strong Python/LLM/data-science content plus an elite name in asset management.

### 3. AstraZeneca
- **Reputation: 10/10** · **Technicality: 5/10** · **Weighted: 8.0**
- **Why:** The single most recognisable name on the list — FTSE-100 global pharma giant, instant credibility everywhere. The project (China supplier-base strategy) is more market-analysis/strategy than ML, though it carries quantitative goals (categorising 50+ suppliers, weighted evaluation frameworks, data-driven recommendations). Ranked high purely on the reputation weighting; choose this if brand leverage matters most.

### 4. Ekimetrics
- **Reputation: 7/10** · **Technicality: 9/10** · **Weighted: 7.8**
- **Why:** Highly respected specialist data-science & AI consultancy (~500 experts) — strong reputation specifically within analytics/BA circles, with a direct alumni network. Two near-identical briefs (Marketing Mix Modelling for a retail client / for an eco-train service): genuine end-to-end data science — Python/R, ML + econometrics, feature engineering, regression modelling, mentored by their data scientists. Excellent technical training value.

### 5. Celonis
- **Reputation: 8/10** · **Technicality: 6/10** · **Weighted: 7.2**
- **Why:** Well-known tech unicorn and the global leader in process mining — a strong, modern name in the data/tech world. The project applies process-mining + AI/ML to an order-management case, but uses a demo dataset and a low-code studio with guided training, so the hands-on coding depth is moderate rather than deep.

### 6. Experian
- **Reputation: 9/10** · **Technicality: 4/10** · **Weighted: 7.0**
- **Why:** Major global data/analytics & credit brand (FTSE 100) — strong CV value, especially for finance/data careers. However, the brief (understanding 18–24-year-olds' financial journeys) is consumer-insight, research and strategy — minimal Python/ML. High purely on the reputation weighting.

### 7. CeramTec GmbH — ⭐ Most Technical Project
- **Reputation: 6/10** · **Technicality: 10/10** · **Weighted: 7.6**
- **Why:** Global leader in advanced ceramics — a solid B2B-industrial name (not a household one). Submitted the **two most technically demanding briefs on the entire list**:
  - *Agentic AI / LLM:* build an autonomous multi-step AI agent over unstructured Salesforce data — LLMs, prompting, embeddings, NLP, hybrid Win-Probability model. **(Recommended brief.)**
  - *Dynamic Pricing feasibility:* Python, pandas, scikit-learn, classification with class imbalance, clustering/segmentation, feature engineering.
- If the goal is the most interesting, cutting-edge ML/DL build, this is the strongest content — held at #7 only because the reputation weighting favours the bigger names above.

### 8. The Design Museum
- **Reputation: 6/10** · **Technicality: 7/10** · **Weighted: 6.4**
- **Why:** Iconic, instantly recognisable London cultural institution — a memorable, distinctive line on a CV (good "story" value), though a small organisation in corporate-leverage terms. The project is a clean, well-scoped ML problem: visitor-demand forecasting with Python/R, multiple modelling approaches, feature/external-variable analysis.

### 9. Royal Avebe
- **Reputation: 6/10** · **Technicality: 6/10** · **Weighted: 6.0**
- **Why:** Large international farmer-owned cooperative ("Royal" designation, sizeable industrial operation) — reasonable scale, lower brand recognition. Technically rich operations-analytics project: queuing theory, forecasting, and simulation on real intake/logistics data — more operations-research than ML/DL, but genuinely quantitative and data-driven.

### 10. MAIA Labs
- **Reputation: 5/10** · **Technicality: 4/10** · **Weighted: 4.6**
- **Why:** Award-winning deep-tech medtech startup ("European MedTech Startup of the Year", AI colonoscopy co-pilot) — a compelling innovation story and AI-product narrative, though small. The project itself is European market-prioritisation/GTM (weighted scoring matrix, forecasts) rather than ML build — included as the strongest of the remaining startups for narrative appeal.

### How to read this
- **Public companies (AstraZeneca, RBC, S&P Global/CRISIL, Experian)** have a true, comparable market cap — the cleanest measure of corporate scale and brand leverage.
- **Celonis & CeramTec** are private: figures are *last-round valuation* and *acquisition enterprise value* respectively — directional, not live market caps, and likely stale.
- **Royal Avebe, Design Museum, Ekimetrics, MAIA Labs** have no market cap at all (cooperative / charity / private firm / startup) — judge these on reputation and project quality, not financial size.
- **Caveat:** all figures are approximate and as of roughly late 2025 / early 2026 (assistant knowledge cutoff). Verify live numbers before relying on them — public market caps move daily.

## Recommendation

- **Best overall (name + technical):** **CRISIL Coalition Greenwich (S&P Global)** and **RBC** — both deliver a prestigious finance brand *and* a heavily technical, data-rich, Python/ML/LLM project.
- **If maximising brand leverage:** **AstraZeneca** or **Experian** — biggest names, but accept a strategy-led, less technical project.
- **If maximising technical/ML depth:** **CeramTec (Agentic AI brief)** or **Ekimetrics** — the deepest hands-on ML/LLM/data-science work, with good (if less famous) reputations.

## Companies Excluded from Top 10 (and why)
Mostly early-stage startups or pure strategy/market-research briefs with limited Python/ML depth and lower brand leverage: Anemocyte, Halo, Heal Earth, illumi One, In Digital Marketing, SynX Therapeutics, Strivolabs (×2), TxODDS, Cyber Square.
